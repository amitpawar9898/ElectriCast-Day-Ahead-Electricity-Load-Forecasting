The script reads the raw apartment CSVs, rebuilds the gap-handled target, and overwrites
`backend/results.json`. No model training is involved at this checkpoint.

## Protocol

- **Timezone:** Asia/Kolkata (IST); every forecast covers 24 IST clock hours.
- **Target:** mean hourly load per reporting stable apartment, in Wh (`target_wh`).
- **Forecast origin:** 00:00 IST of each test day.
- **Information set:** target observations strictly before the origin. Lag baselines use
  lags of 24 h or longer, so none of them reads a value from the forecast day.
- **Folds:** four expanding windows with `train_start` fixed at 2017-05-01.
- **Evaluation mask:** hours where the target and the seasonal-naive-24 forecast both
  exist, so all baselines are scored on the same hours within a fold.
- **Metrics:** MAE, RMSE, WAPE (%), MASE, bias, R², sMAPE (%). MASE scale is the mean
  absolute 24-h seasonal difference over valid training pairs in the fold.
- **Best baseline:** lowest MASE on the all-four-baseline mask when that mask covers at
  least max(24 h, 50%) of the evaluation hours; otherwise on the evaluation mask.
- **Significance:** two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test on paired absolute errors against
  the best baseline. p-values are unadjusted; `small_sample` flags fewer than 10 pairs.

## How to read this

- No learned model exists yet; these are baseline-only numbers and will be the reference
  for every model in Phase 4. A model must be trained on the fold's train window and beat
  the best baseline on the same evaluation mask.
- In the two dense summer folds the best baseline is a seasonal/calendar average; in the
  heterogeneous Sep–Dec fold plain persistence wins, and in the sparse 2018 fold seasonal
  naive wins.

## Limitations

- Only four folds, and the 2018 fold has 182 evaluable hours across 8 days; results there
  are indicative, not precise.
- The stable panel covers 24 apartments and the target is a per-reporting-apartment mean,
  not total building load. December 2017 includes suspected vacancies (21 apartments
  flagged inactive in data quality), which affects the Sep–Dec fold.
- The Sep–Dec selection mask (1,290 h) is denser than its evaluation mask (2,243 h), so
  the best baseline was chosen on a different subset than the headline metrics; the JSON
  records both masks and their metrics.
- Wilcoxon p-values are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. They compare one baseline
  pair at a time and are not a model-selection procedure.
- The models so far use only the target history. Calendar, weather, and learned models
  arrive in Phase 4; until then the UI must keep presenting baseline results as baselines.
